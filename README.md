# FINITE — your one life, live

A progressive web app that turns *memento mori* into a live dashboard.

Enter your birth date once. FINITE then shows you, computed entirely on your
device and updating in real time:

- **Now** — your heartbeats, breaths, seconds alive, and the kilometres you've
  travelled around the Sun (you're doing 29.78 km/s right now), all ticking live.
- **One day** — your whole life compressed into a single 24-hour clock.
  Midnight is birth, the next midnight is your horizon, and the clock never
  stops. *"It is now 14:32 in your life."*
- **The map** — every week of your life as one dot on one screen
  (~4,700 of them). Spent weeks burn ember; the bright pulsing dot is this
  week. Tap any dot to time-travel.
- **What remains** — the arithmetic no one does: sunrises, Saturdays, summers,
  full moons, books you'll still read, and the hardest number of all — how many
  more times you may see the people you love.
- **Share** — generates a share card of your week map (Web Share API).

## Privacy

There is no server, no account, no analytics, and no network call after the
first load. Your birth date lives in `localStorage` on your own device.

## Install on Android

Open the deployed URL in Chrome → tap the **Install** button in the top bar
(or Chrome menu → *Add to Home screen*). It runs full-screen, offline,
like a native app.

## Hosting

Pushing to `main` triggers `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`, which enables and
deploys GitHub Pages automatically — the app goes live at
<https://vigneshbhaskarraj.github.io/finite/> with no manual setup.

## Stack

Zero dependencies, zero build step: vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, a canvas-rendered
week map, a service worker for offline, and PNG icons generated by a
dependency-free Node script (`tools/make-icons.mjs` — it even implements its
own PNG encoder).
